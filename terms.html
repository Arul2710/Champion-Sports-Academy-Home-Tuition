Clause One

Acceptance Of These Terms

Please read these terms before enrolling an athlete.

Agreement to terms

By enrolling an athlete in a Champion Sports Academy programme, submitting an enrollment form, or creating a parent portal account, you confirm that you have read and agree to be bound by these terms on behalf of the athlete.

If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please do not enroll. Contact our office and we will answer any questions first.

Who these terms cover

These terms apply to parents and guardians, athletes aged 5 to 17, and any adult accompanying an athlete to sessions.

Athletes aged 18 and over enrolling independently accept these terms in their own right.

Champion Sports Academy may update these terms. Material changes will be announced at least 14 days before taking effect.

Clause Two

Enrollment & Fees

How bookings, payments, changes and cancellations work.

Bookings and placements

Places are confirmed once payment is received and the coach has completed a level assessment.

  • Group sizes are capped to protect coach-to-player ratios
  • Level placement is at the coach’s discretion after assessment
  • A free trial session does not guarantee a place in a particular batch
  • Kit is issued on the first paid session

Fees, payment and changes

Fees are charged monthly in advance and cover all scheduled sessions in that month.

  • Fees are refundable pro-rata with two weeks written notice
  • Tournament entry fees are non-refundable once a place is confirmed
  • Batches may be paused for up to eight weeks per season on notice
  • Changes take effect from the start of the following month
  • Two consecutive missed payments may release your place
Clause Three

Conduct, Safety & Supervision

Our expectations of athletes, parents and staff.

Athlete conduct

Athletes are expected to:

  • Listen to coaches and follow safety instructions
  • Treat teammates, opponents and staff with respect
  • Attend regularly and arrive on time
  • Refuse to participate if unwell or injured

Parent responsibilities

Parents agree to:

  • Ensure athletes attend with appropriate clothing and footwear
  • Update us promptly of any medical change or injury
  • Collect athletes promptly after sessions
  • Not photograph or record sessions without consent

Health and liability

Training carries an inherent risk of injury. By enrolling, you accept that risk.

We hold appropriate public liability insurance and every staff member is first-aid certified. Mandatory medical clearance is required before an athlete may compete in tournaments. Any injury must be reported to the coach immediately.

Complaints Procedure

  1. 1 Raise the concern with your child’s coach at the session.
  2. 2 If unresolved, contact the academy manager in writing within 14 days.
  3. 3 The manager will respond with a written outcome within 10 working days.
  4. 4 If still unresolved, the matter escalates to the academy director for a final review.
