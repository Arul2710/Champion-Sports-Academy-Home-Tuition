Legal

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and protect your personal information as a parent or athlete at Champion Sports Academy.

Contact Our Team Terms of Service
Section One

Information We Collect

We only collect what we need to coach your child and run the academy.

Information you provide

Details you give us directly when you enroll, contact us, or create a parent account:

  • Parent or guardian name, email address and phone number
  • Athlete name, date of birth and age group
  • Emergency contact details
  • Medical and accessibility information you choose to share
  • Payment and billing records

Information collected automatically

Standard technical data gathered when you browse the site or use the parent dashboard:

  • Pages viewed and approximate timestamp
  • Device type, browser and screen size
  • Approximate region derived from IP address
  • Session and preference settings such as theme and text direction
Section Two

How We Use Your Information

Every use of your data is tied to running the academy safely.

Delivering our service

To manage your enrollment and keep your child safe:

  • Assign athletes to the correct age and ability group
  • Share session plans and progress notes with your child’s coach
  • Operate the parent dashboard, attendance records and fee schedules
  • Handle emergency contacts and medical information

Communications

We contact you about your child’s training:

  • Batch confirmations, schedule changes and venue updates
  • Fee reminders and payment receipts
  • Tournament and competition notifications
  • Occasional academy news, only if you opt in
Section Three

Sharing, Storage & Retention

We do not sell your data. Here is exactly who can see it.

Who we share with

Your information is visible only where it is genuinely needed:

  • Your child’s assigned coach and hub staff
  • Our payment processor, for handling fees
  • Competition organisers, only for tournament entry
  • Regulators or authorities, where legally required

How it is stored

Your information is held in our secure booking and parent portal systems, with access restricted by role. Paper records, where they exist, are kept in locked filing cabinets at our head office and destroyed once no longer required.

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections

    • How long we keep it

    Retention periods reflect both academy and legal requirements:

    • Enrolment and attendance records: 7 years
    • Payment and billing records: 7 years
    • Medical and safeguarding records: 7 years
    • Marketing preferences: until you unsubscribe
    Section Four

    Your Rights

    You stay in control of your information at all times.

    • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
    • Ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate
    • Request deletion of data we are not legally required to keep
    • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
    • Object to how your data is used in a particular context

    To exercise any of these rights, contact our office and we will respond within 30 days.

    Questions About Your Data?

    Our operations team handles every privacy request personally. Get in touch and we will help with access, correction or deletion.
